# Client Brand & Visual Compliance

## Overview
Pre-delivery verification checklist ensuring client visual identity, exact brand guidelines, and official color standards across DCC render pipelines (3ds Max, Blender, Unreal Engine).

## Verification Guidelines
1. **Official Color Palette**:
   - Intermarine Official Logo: Dark Navy Blue (`#001f3f` / `#003366` / `#0055ff`).
   - Strictly prohibit unapproved teal, cyan, or grey substitutions on client-facing deliverables.
2. **Asset Inspection**:
   - Cross-check all render previews and promotional stills before sending to client.
   - Verify logo placement, aspect ratio preservation, and clear-space margins.
3. **Delivery Quality Signoff**:
   - Quality Gate validation must confirm official brand compliance before export signoff.
